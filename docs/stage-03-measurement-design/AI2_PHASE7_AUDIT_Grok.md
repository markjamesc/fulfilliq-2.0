## (1) Pass items

**Hypothesis clarity / falsifiability.** The decision hypothesis (material, repeated customer lateness plus an observable fulfillment-process signal) is operationalized as three independently fail-able measurement hypotheses, with H4 correctly out of scope. H1–H3 map onto named gates rather than narrative judgment. H2 is now explicit that the half test is **parity-or-above** (`LFR ≥` that half’s comparator; equality passes), not strict elevation, and that half counts do **not** rule out a single episode that straddles the April–May boundary. H3 is explicit that half-repetition applies only to customer lateness and that handoff is a **full-window** membership test, not a half-repeated requirement. Failure of H1–H3 is framed as insufficient support under this rule, not proof of acceptable performance. Competing explanations in §6 are listed; B’s band-P75 + market LFR + Wilson LB path remains rejected as the wrong question.

**Construct validity (primary).** Seller LFR is a single construct: among eligible delivered seller–orders, late iff `DATE(order_delivered_customer_date) > DATE(order_estimated_delivery_date)`; same-day on time; **no grace period**; missing outcomes never coded on-time; nondelivered orders excluded from the primary denominator. The fraction `late_n/eligible_n` is mandatory beside any display rate. The date/timestamp twin plus N5 (action compared **after** capacity selection) treats clock semantics as a construct threat with a specified override, not as an informal sensitivity. Handoff is defined as carrier receipt **strictly later than** the latest item `shipping_limit_date` on single-seller late orders, and is labeled a proxy that does **not** prove seller responsibility. Ship-limit/carrier **summaries** are separated from the §11.2 membership path.

**Population alignment.** Reporting universe is every validated `seller_id` in `raw_sellers`, including zero-eligible and tiny-volume sellers, with reason codes. Tiny-volume rule matches the locked membership intent: after all eligibility exclusions, `eligible_n < 30` → cannot qualify / standard terms (floor **≥ 30**, PA). Featured placement / catalog remains a business exclusion regardless of PENDING-DB schema outcome. Window is the locked B calendar: purchase `>= '2018-01-01 00:00:00'` and `< '2018-09-01 00:00:00'`; non-overlapping halves Jan–Apr / May–Aug; cohort clock = purchase time; post-window delivery still counts if purchase is in window. Membership-first, then rank only qualifiers; **C = 20**; `S = max(0, C − O − R)`; select `min(S, Q)`; **no padding**; if fewer meet the bar, enroll fewer. Simulation `S = C` is allowed only when labeled full-capacity simulation and is not an operational-release packet.

**Denominator validity.** Eligible seller–order rules are conjunctive and additive (valid association, delivered, in-window purchase, parseable actual and estimated delivery, chronology not before purchase, ≥1 contributing item). Grain is `(seller_id, order_id)` after collapsing items; multi-seller orders contribute one association each with a **shared** order-level outcome and a carried `multi_seller_order_flag`. Coverage uses `eligible / candidate_delivered ≥ 95%` (zero candidate → fail) as a measurement-completeness gate, not as a substitute late definition. Primary path excludes payments, reviews, products, and raw geolocation.

**Comparison logic.** The assessable reference is sellers with `eligible_n ≥ 30` **and** passing coverage—not conditioned on high lateness, repetition, or handoff. The baseline is seller-excluded leave-one-out  
\(p_{-s}=\sum_{j\neq s}k_j/\sum_{j\neq s}n_j\)  
on that reference. Usability cutoffs are controlling: full-window **≥ 20** other comparator sellers **and ≥ 1,000** comparator eligible seller–orders; each half **≥ 200** comparator eligible seller–orders. Failed usability fails the dependent materiality/persistence gates (cannot reach YES). Context metrics (marketplace LFR, unique-order LFR, unweighted median seller LFR, volume concentration) are labeled non-interchangeable. B’s volume-band P75 + weighted market LFR + Wilson 80% LB is rejected as a qualify rule.

**Confounding / attribution.** Multi-seller shared clocks have a fully specified N4 (flip branch; thin + majority branch; ss volume floor `eligible_n_ss < 30`; majority `eligible_n_ms/eligible_n > 0.5` **or** `late_n_ms/late_n > 0.5`; ss LOO and ss half persistence recomputed; ss comparator unusable → ss path not rate-clearing). N4 cannot upgrade an all-order failure. Promise tightness and severity (`> 7` calendar days late) cannot enroll. Carrier/network/field-unsuitability is separated into an operational-release hold (§14.1) that does **not** rewrite calculated membership. Lookahead is constrained to historical snapshot scoring and disclosed (L001). No regression adjustment replaces the deterministic rule. Analyst discretion is forbidden as a membership substitute.

**Segment multiplicity.** Required segments (volume bands **0 / 1–9 / 10–29 / 30–99 / ≥ 100**, month, halves, single- vs multi-seller, coverage, handoff availability) are interpretive only and do not change primary numerator/denominator or add eligibility thresholds. Sparse-cell rule: retain counts; **30** observations provisional only for displayed segment-rate comparisons.

**Sample-size and uncertainty handling.** Decision floors are stated as PA policy cutoffs serving enrollment, not as power claims: qualify **≥ 30** eligible; **≥ 5** late; halves **≥ 10** eligible and **≥ 2** late; handoff **≥ 3** supporting, supporting **≥ 50%** of all eligible late, **≥ 90%** of single-seller late evaluable, zero single-seller late fails. Wilson **95%** is descriptive only and is not an enrollment gate. Ordinary sensitivities (floor **20** and **50** vs **30**; elevation **2 pp** and **5 pp** vs **3**; coverage **90%** and **99%** vs **95%**; handoff share **1/3** and **2/3** vs **1/2**) do **not** override primary action. N5 is the sole prespecified sensitivity with action-override authority. Exact integer/rational comparisons are required for gates and ranking; displayed percentages are not decision inputs.

**Causal ceiling.** Allowed output is associational/descriptive membership and a prospective recommendation table. Forbidden: treatment effects, “enrollment will reduce late deliveries,” proof of fault, WATCH as a second enrollment track, and any Stage 3 use of `seller_plan_enrollment` as a contrast. Action taxonomy is proportional to the locked decision: only `ENROLL_RECOMMENDED` is an enrollment recommendation; `WATCH` and `INCONCLUSIVE` remain on standard terms operationally; `STANDARD_NOT_QUALIFIED` covers tiny-volume and failed gates. Non-RCT is disclosed.

**Proportionality of the locked rule stack (where specified).** Ranking applies only to post-N4/N5 YES members, in a fixed order (descending excess burden \(e_s = k_s - n_s p_{-s}\), then `handoff_support_n`, `late_n`, `eligible_n`, ascending `seller_id`). Excess-burden ranking is aligned with “reduce late customer deliveries” (volume-weighted extra late orders) rather than rate-only vanity ranking. No fill-to-cap, no automatic top-twenty, no severity-only override, no capacity-driven threshold relaxation. N5 refill is single-pass under Run D only; disagreers stay `INCONCLUSIVE` and are never refilled to enroll.

**Cutoffs as specified (all decision numerics PA unless marked structural LOCK):** window 2018-01-01 incl. → 2018-09-01 excl. (LOCK); halves Jan–Apr / May–Aug (LOCK); **C = 20** (LOCK integer of “about 20”); `eligible_n < 30` cannot qualify; coverage **≥ 95%**; `late_n ≥ 5` and LFR **at least 3 pp** above \(p_{-s}\) (equality on the 3 pp boundary passes); each half **≥ 10** eligible, **≥ 2** late, LFR **≥** half comparator (equality passes); handoff **≥ 3** support, **≥ 50%** of all eligible late, **≥ 90%** ss-late evaluable, 0 ss late fails; comparator **≥ 20** other sellers and **≥ 1,000** eligible orders full-window, **≥ 200** eligible orders each half; N4 ss floor **< 30**; N4 majority **> 0.5**.

## (2) Remaining methodological risks

These are residual threats under the locked spine. They do not by themselves require new cutoffs, a new hypothesis hierarchy, or a rewrite of the approved decision/question.

- **H2 is a weak test of “temporary episode.”** Halves require only **≥ 2** late and **≥ 10** eligible, and allow **parity** with the half comparator. Two late orders in a 4-month block can be one cluster. The design correctly discloses that a single episode straddling 1 May can still pass. Competing explanation “temporary episode” is therefore only partially probed. Stronger anti-straddle or within-half spacing tests would be a **new** approved test; they are not implied by current A/B integers.

- **H3 process-signal need not persist.** All `handoff_support_n` events may sit in one half and still pass fit. Combined with weak H2, a seller can show full-window 3 pp elevation, half parity-or-above on thin late counts, and a one-sided handoff cluster. That is internally consistent with the written H3, but it is a thin warrant for a documented seller-corrective path if the intended construct is *repeated process failure*.

- **Handoff 50% denominator vs ss-only numerator.** Supporting share uses **all eligible late** as denominator while support events can only arise from single-seller evaluable lates. High multi-seller late share can fail fit even when ss handoff is strong; zero ss late fails fit even when all-order H1/H2 would pass. Attribution is then coded as handoff failure (`STANDARD_NOT_QUALIFIED`) rather than N4 `INCONCLUSIVE`. Coherent, but the reason code will not always match the substantive threat (shared clocks).

- **Volume-weighted LOO is dominated by the right tail.** VF context: median delivered volume **6**; **79.74%** of sellers have fewer than 30 (full-extract profile, not locked-window headcount). The reference is already `eligible_n ≥ 30` plus coverage, so \(p_{-s}\) is a large-seller pooled rate. Unweighted median seller LFR is context only. Elevation of **3 pp** vs that pool is a peer-mix construct, not a stable marketplace parameter. Near the floor, `late_n ≥ 5` often binds before 3 pp (e.g., 5/30 ≈ 16.7%).

- **Delivered-only survivorship and promise mix remain in the estimand.** LFR is late among completed deliveries versus the promised date. Undelivered/canceled work is outside the primary denominator; tight vs loose estimates can inflate LFR without a handoff miss. Both are disclosed; neither is removed. Fit is a proxy, not seller fault.

- **Comparator usability can zero the YES set.** If the locked window cannot supply **≥ 20** other assessable sellers and **≥ 1,000** comparator eligible orders (or **≥ 200** per half), every materiality/persistence-dependent seller fails. That is specified correctly (cannot YES) but is an operational emptiness risk, not a robustness result.

- **N4 “ss volume contributing” for comparator usability is slightly underspecified.** Reference membership stays all-order `eligible_n ≥ 30` + coverage. The ss usability clause requires “≥ 20 other reference sellers **with ss volume contributing**” plus **≥ 1,000 / ≥ 200** ss eligible orders. “Contributing” is not pinned to `eligible_n_ss > 0` versus some other floor. Implementers could diverge on the seller-count part of ss usability without changing listed integers.

- **Point-estimate ranking at the cap margin.** Selection uses exact \(e_s\) with no uncertainty penalty. Wilson intervals do not affect rank. Sellers 20 vs 21 under a given **S** can swap from sampling noise in \(k_s\) or in \(p_{-s}\). Acceptable for a deterministic ops rule; residual at the last seat.

- **Historical 2018 snapshot ≠ live mid-month roster.** Retrospective final-observed-status scoring cannot represent what was knowable on a decision day or current seller state. L001 / operational-release hold cover reuse; they do not repair the time gap.

- **Stacked PA gates may yield Q ≪ S.** That matches “enroll fewer; no padding,” so it is not a design contradiction. It does mean empty or near-empty `ENROLL_RECOMMENDED` lists are a foreseeable descriptive outcome, not evidence that “no late-fulfillment problem exists.”

- **Provisional cutoffs are policy, not validated optima.** **30 / 5 / 3 pp / 10 / 2 / 95% / 50% / 90% / 3 / 20 / 1,000 / 200** remain uncalibrated against any outcome of the 30-day plan. Human Design Gate still owns them.

---

## (3) Verdict

**Pass.**

No required fixes that stay inside the locked decision, locked analytical question, Phase 4 AM spine, and “no new thresholds beyond Designs A/B integers” rule. Remaining issues in §2 are accepted-limitation / residual-threat items already largely disclosed (H2 straddle, full-window-only H3, delivered-only construct, LOO mix, L001, PA status).

Optional tightening that is **not** a Revise condition and must not invent cutoffs: in §17.4, state that “ss volume contributing” means `eligible_n_ss > 0` (or the analogous half count > 0) among reference members when applying the existing **≥ 20 / ≥ 1,000 / ≥ 200** integers. That removes an implementer fork without changing any locked number, membership hierarchy, or action taxonomy.

Stage 4 should not proceed past human Design Gate until authoritative **S** (or an explicit full-capacity simulation label) and handoff-field verification / operational-release posture are recorded. Those are lock-approval and readiness items already in the candidate, not measurement-rule defects.
